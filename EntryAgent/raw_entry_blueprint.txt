## Step 5 - Leg 2 Confirmation

Precondition:

- Rejection Mode = ON.
- Leg 1 valid.
- Interaction active.

Step 5 priority order:

- Evaluate 5.3B first, then 5.3A, then 5.1.
- Highest active override always controls.
- Only one Step 5 confirmation path may be active at a time.

5.1 Core Requirement:

A single candle, Leg 2, must:

- Close beyond the active Leg 1 reference.
- Sweep the active Leg 1 extreme.

5.2 Leg 1 Definitions:

- Leg 1 reference = Candle A close by default.
- Leg 1 extreme = most extreme price of Candle A + B.
- These values are fixed once Leg 1 completes, except for the Candle B Reference Upgrade Rule in 5.2A.

5.2A Candle B Reference Upgrade Rule:

Activation:

- Candle B trades beyond Candle A close in the active rejection direction.
- Trade means wick or close.

SHORT, S/R:

- If Candle B high > Candle A close:
  - Candle B becomes the reference candle.
  - Active Leg 1 reference = Candle B close.

LONG, R/S:

- If Candle B low < Candle A close:
  - Candle B becomes the reference candle.
  - Active Leg 1 reference = Candle B close.

Important:

- Candle B does not need to own the extreme.
- Leg 1 extreme remains unchanged unless Candle B actually exceeds Candle A extreme.
- Candle A may still own the extreme while Candle B owns the reference.
- Active path remains Standard 5.1 unless another override activates.

5.3 Extreme Ownership:

- Leg 1 is Candle A plus Candle B.

Case 1, Candle A owns the Leg 1 extreme:

- Candle A remains the most extreme price.
- Candle B does not exceed Candle A extreme.
- Apply standard Leg 2 logic.
- Leg 2 must close beyond active Leg 1 reference and sweep active Leg 1 extreme.

Case 2, Candle B owns the Leg 1 extreme:

- Candle B extends beyond Candle A and becomes the new extreme.
- Still apply standard Leg 2 logic unless override applies.
- Leg 2 must close beyond active Leg 1 reference and sweep Candle B extreme.

Critical clarification:

- Leg 1 reference may only change through 5.2A.
- Reference = Candle A close by default, Candle B close if 5.2A activates.
- Extreme = most extreme of A + B.

5.3A Participation Candle Extreme Override:

If Candle B establishes the Leg 1 extreme, standard Leg 2 sweep logic is overridden.

SHORT, S/R:

- If Candle A owns the extreme: Leg 2 must close beyond active Leg 1 reference and sweep active Leg 1 extreme.
- If Candle B owns the extreme: Leg 2 must close above Candle B high.
- This close alone becomes required confirmation for Leg 2.

LONG, R/S:

- If Candle A owns the extreme: Leg 2 must close beyond active Leg 1 reference and sweep active Leg 1 extreme.
- If Candle B owns the extreme: Leg 2 must close below Candle B low.
- This close alone becomes required confirmation for Leg 2.

Override Priority Rule:

- 5.3A overrides 5.1 whenever its activation condition is met.
- If Candle B establishes the Leg 1 extreme, 5.1 standard Leg 2 logic is disabled.
- Only 5.3A confirmation logic may be used.
- 5.1 may not be blended with 5.3A in the same evaluation cycle.

5.3B Wick Probe Override:

If after Leg 1 is valid, but before a valid Leg 2 close occurs, price wicks beyond the active Leg 1 extreme:

- That wick becomes the new confirmation threshold for Leg 2.
- Wick beyond Leg 1 extreme does not validate Leg 2 by itself.
- It creates a Wick Probe that must later be accepted by close.

LONG, R/S:

- If price wicks below active Leg 1 extreme but does not close below active Leg 1 reference:
  - Leg 2 = NOT VALID.
  - Wick Probe = ACTIVE.
  - Lowest wick beyond Leg 1 extreme becomes Probe Low.
  - Valid Leg 2 then requires close beyond active Leg 1 reference and close below lowest active Probe Low.

SHORT, S/R:

- If price wicks above active Leg 1 extreme but does not close beyond active Leg 1 reference:
  - Leg 2 = NOT VALID.
  - Wick Probe = ACTIVE.
  - Highest wick beyond Leg 1 extreme becomes Probe High.
  - Valid Leg 2 then requires close beyond active Leg 1 reference and close above highest active Probe High.

Probe update rule:

- Additional LONG probes use the lowest Probe Low.
- Additional SHORT probes use the highest Probe High.
- Active probe threshold must always represent the most extreme failed wick probe.

Override Priority Rule:

- 5.3B overrides both 5.1 and 5.3A whenever its activation condition is met.
- If valid Wick Probe forms after Leg 1 completion and before valid Leg 2 confirmation, all prior Leg 2 confirmation logic is suspended.
- Only 5.3B confirmation logic may be used until Leg 2 confirms or the interaction terminates.
- 5.3B may not be blended with 5.1 or 5.3A.

5.4 Dynamic Stack Routing:

- If dynamic stacking becomes active after Leg 1 validation, Leg 2 confirmation must include standard Leg 2 requirements plus sweep of next liquidity Extreme Boundary.
- Failure to sweep that boundary makes Leg 2 invalid.

5.5 Outcome:

- If all conditions are met, 2-Leg Structure = CONFIRMED and proceed to Step 6.
- If any condition fails, Structure = INVALID and proceed to Step 7.

5.6 Anchor Extreme + Opposite-Side Close Invalidation:

Anchor Extreme:

- High or low of the displacement candle that establishes rejection setup.
- Final aggressive push into liquidity before opposing side begins to take control.

For SHORT, S/R:

- Anchor Extreme = HIGH of final bullish displacement candle into liquidity.

For LONG, R/S:

- Anchor Extreme = LOW of final bearish displacement candle into liquidity.

Qualification:

- Anchor Extreme candle must be part of a displacement move, not chop.
- It must be the final push into liquidity.
- It must represent the point where continuation fails.

Invalidation:

- Once Anchor Extreme is established, price must not close beyond that extreme on the opposite side before Step 6 entry completes.
- If price closes beyond Anchor Extreme:
  - Structure = INVALID.
  - Rejection Mode = OFF.
  - Proceed to Step 7.

Interpretation:

- Wicks beyond Anchor Extreme are allowed.
- Only a close beyond Anchor Extreme invalidates setup.
- Closes within liquidity or Leg 1 structure do not invalidate.

Invalidation priority:

- Anchor Extreme Close always takes priority.
- Opposing Setup Override is evaluated only if Anchor Extreme has not been violated.

5.6A Failed Leg 2 Resolution:

If price sweeps active Leg 1 extreme but does not close beyond active Leg 1 reference:

- Leg 2 is invalid.

If after failed Leg 2 attempt, price closes back through liquidity in the opposite direction of the rejection attempt:

- Rejection Mode = OFF.
- Current rejection sequence = TERMINATED.
- Proceed to Step 7.

5.7 Active Reference Rule:

- Step 5 must use only one active structural reference.
- Candle A close by default.
- Candle B close if 5.2A activates.
- The active Leg 1 reference may not be redefined, blended, or replaced after Leg 1 completes.
- Leg 1 extreme remains separate from Leg 1 reference.
- Leg 2 always confirms beyond Leg 1.

Anchor Stability Rule:

- Once assigned, Anchor Extreme may not be changed, updated, or reassigned within the same interaction.
- All validation, invalidation, and entry protection must reference original Anchor Extreme only.
- No recalculation or reinterpretation allowed.

## Step 6 - Entry Types

First valid trigger wins.

6.1 Opposing Setup Override:

If a valid opposing rejection setup forms before the current setup completes, original setup is immediately invalidated.

Condition:

- During active rejection sequence, opposing S/R or R/S setup completes Leg 1 and begins to resolve price in opposite direction.

Then:

- Original setup is invalidated.
- All Leg 1 / Leg 2 structures tied to original setup are discarded.
- System immediately abandons original setup.
- Control transfers to opposing setup.
- Opposing setup becomes only valid pathway.

Core law:

- Only one directional premise may remain valid within an interaction.

6.2 SC Progression + Entry Re-Evaluation:

- Initial Signal Candle, SC, is Leg 2 candle.
- If SC does not trigger entry, subsequent candle closing beyond SC close in setup direction may become SC2.
- If SC2 does not trigger entry, subsequent candle closing beyond SC2 close in setup direction may become SC3.
- The next candle after SC3 must trigger valid entry.
- If it does not, Structure = INVALID and proceed to Step 7.

Direction:

- LONG: close above current SC close.
- SHORT: close below current SC close.

SC reassignment applies only to Step 6 entry logic.

SC-Based Entry Re-Evaluation:

- Each SC, SC2, SC3 is evaluated independently.
- When SC updates, all entry models are re-evaluated from scratch.
- Prior eliminations are discarded.

Hard boundary:

- Applies only to Step 6 entry logic.
- Does not affect Step 5 structure, Leg 1 / Leg 2, timing window, or interaction state.

Active reference:

- Only current SC is used for wick measurement, body reclaim levels, and displacement calculations.
- Previous SCs are not referenced, blended, or reused.

6.3 SC Decision Pass:

Before evaluating next candle, classify all allowed entry models for the active SC.

Step 1, Wick Classification:

- Measure SC wick as percentage of total SC range.
- If SC wick >= 20%:
  - Sweep Entry = Large Wick path.
  - Double Wick = ACTIVE.
- If SC wick < 20%:
  - Sweep Entry = Small Wick path.
  - Double Wick = ELIMINATED.

Step 2, Active Model Output:

Must state:

- Sweep Entry path = Large Wick or Small Wick.
- Double Wick = ACTIVE or ELIMINATED.

Reset rule:

- Whenever SC updates, all prior model eligibility is discarded.
- Full SC Decision Pass recomputed from scratch using new SC only.

Enforcement:

- No entry evaluation may occur until SC Decision Pass output is explicitly defined.

6.3X Required Leg-In Liquidity Sweep Gate, S/R and R/S only:

Applies only to:

- S/R continuation setups.
- R/S continuation setups.

Does not apply to:

- Standard rejection setups.
- Any non S/R / R/S pathway.

Required leg-in liquidity exists only when:

- Controlling Mode = S/R or R/S.
- The same leg that qualified the setup, wick or close beyond level, also created internal liquidity before reaching the level.

S/R, SHORT:

- If the leg that wicked into LL or closed below LL also created a Higher High before reaching LL, that HH = required liquidity.

R/S, LONG:

- If the leg that wicked into LH or closed above LH also created a Lower Low before reaching LH, that LL = required liquidity.

Rule:

- If controlling_mode is S/R or R/S and required leg-in liquidity exists, entry evaluation is blocked until that liquidity is swept.

If not swept:

- Step 6 = WAIT.
- Interaction = ACTIVE.
- Structure = VALID.
- SC progression continues.
- No entry model may trigger.

If swept:

- Continue to 6.3A / 6.3B entry evaluation.

Failure:

- If SC progression expires before sweep, Entry Failure and proceed to Step 7.

Important:

- This rule only gates Step 6.
- It does not invalidate Step 4 or Step 5.
- It does not reset interaction.
- This rule is active only when controlling_mode = S/R or R/S.
- Otherwise, ignore completely.

Entry Model 1, Sweep Entry:

Large Wick Sweep:

- Condition: SC wick >= 20% total SC range.
- SHORT wick = SC high to SC body high.
- LONG wick = SC body low to SC low.
- Reclaim measured across wick only.

Requirements:

- Entry candle sweeps SC extreme by at least 1 tick.
- Reclaims at least 60% of SC wick range.
- Reclaim level exceeded by at least 1 tick.
- Only first valid reclaim after sweep may trigger entry.

SHORT trigger:

- Wick Range = SC high to SC body high.
- 60% level = 60% of wick measured down from SC high.
- Price trades back below 60% reclaim level by at least 1 tick.

LONG trigger:

- Wick Range = SC body low to SC low.
- 60% level = 60% of wick measured up from SC low.
- Price trades back above 60% reclaim level by at least 1 tick.

Execution:

- Intrabar valid.
- No close required.
- First valid touch through reclaim level = trigger.

Invalidation:

- If sweep occurs but reclaim does not reach required wick level cleanly, no Large Wick Sweep entry exists.
- Repeated reclaim attempts on same SC are invalid.

Small Wick Sweep:

- Condition: SC wick < 20% total SC range.

Requirements:

- Sweep SC extreme by at least 1 tick.
- Reclaim SC body level by at least 1 tick.
- Occurs within SC progression window.
- Only first valid reclaim after sweep may trigger entry.

SC body:

- BODY LOW = lowest open/close.
- BODY HIGH = highest open/close.

LONG:

- Sweep below SC low.
- Trigger when price trades above BODY LOW by at least 1 tick.

SHORT:

- Sweep above SC high.
- Trigger when price trades below BODY HIGH by at least 1 tick.

Execution:

- Intrabar valid.
- No close required.
- First valid touch through body reclaim level = trigger.

Invalidation:

- If sweep occurs but reclaim does not exceed body level by at least 1 tick, no Small Wick Sweep entry exists.
- Repeated reclaim attempts on same SC are invalid.

Entry Model 2, Double Wick Rejection:

- SC = candle immediately before entry.
- SC wick >= 20% of total SC range.
- Entry penetrates at least 50% of SC wick.
- Entry reclaims.

SHORT:

- Penetrate above.
- Reclaim below.

LONG:

- Penetrate below.
- Reclaim above.

Execution:

- Intrabar valid.
- No close required.

6.3A Model Enforcement:

- Uses SC Decision Pass output.
- No independent classification occurs.
- Per current SC only.
- Elimination logic applied fresh using active SC.
- Sweep Entry always remains if conditions possible.
- When SC updates, all elimination logic is cleared and recomputed.
- If 2 models eliminated, remaining model is active.

6.4 Entry Confirmation + Routing:

If any model triggers:

- ENTRY CONFIRMED.
- EXECUTE IMMEDIATELY.
- Interaction = CONSUMED.

State changes:

- Rejection Mode = OFF.
- Trade Mode = OFF.
- Structure = LOCKED.

Structure lock:

- No changes to Leg 1, Leg 2, or SC.

Single Entry Rule:

- Only one entry allowed.

System transition:

- ACTIVE TRADE STATE.
- Proceed to Step 10.

Post entry:

- No new evaluation.
- No continuation.
- No new interactions.

After trade resolution:

- NEUTRAL.

Core law:

- Entry consumes interaction.

6.4a Entry Time Priority:

- All active entry models must be evaluated simultaneously.
- Execute first model that becomes valid in time.
- No later-valid model may override or delay earlier-valid trigger.
- If multiple models qualify within same candle, earliest intrabar trigger takes precedence.

6.5 Invalidation:

Structure invalid if:

- No entry within SC -> SC2 -> SC3 -> next candle.
- No valid trigger.
- Sweep without reclaim.

Then proceed to Step 7.

6.6 Global Invalidation:

- No trigger.
- Timing fails.
- Structure breaks.
- No trade.

6.7 Mode Failure:

- If price closes beyond Anchor Extreme before entry is triggered:
  - Structure = INVALID.
  - Proceed to Step 7.
- Closes within liquidity or Leg 1 structure do not count as failure.
- Only Anchor Extreme violation defines true opposite-side failure.

6.8 System Truth:

- Step 4 = Participation.
- Step 5 = Structure Confirmation.
- Step 6 = Entry.

6.9 Core Interpretation:

- SC is active anchor.
- SC may update to SC2 / SC3.
- Only current SC is used.
- Entry models re-evaluated per SC.

6.10 Rejection Completion:

- Rejection sequence Steps 3-6 must fully complete for a trade to be valid.
- If any part fails, becomes invalid, or does not produce valid entry:
  - Rejection attempt is FAILED.
  - No trade may be taken from that interaction.
  - Interaction is complete or terminated per global rules.

Final bank line:

- Structure stays fixed. SC adapts. Entry must happen immediately or setup is dead.

6.11 Leg 2 Re-Attempt Model, BE-Based:

Activation requires all:

- Trade was executed from Step 6.
- Trade reached Break Even.
- Structure has not been invalidated.
- No opposite-side close has occurred.
- Interaction is still active.
- Step 7 has not triggered.
- Rule 4A.0 does not apply here.

Transformation:

- Once BE is triggered, New Leg 1 close = original entry price.
- New Leg 1 extreme = original entry extreme used for stop.
- No new participation is created.
- No new Step 4 is evaluated.

New Leg 2 requirement:

- Sweep new Leg 1 extreme.
- Close beyond new Leg 1 close.

Entry:

- Same Step 6 rules apply.
- SC progression active.

Timing:

- Must complete within 3 candles from BE trigger.
- If not, invalid and proceed to Step 7.

Hard limits:

- Maximum one re-attempt per interaction.
- If re-attempt fails, interaction is complete.
- Cannot occur if full stop was hit, structure breaks, or opposite-side close occurs.

Risk:

- Treated as new trade.
- Must respect 10% max risk.
- No overlapping exposure allowed.

Core law:

- If the market defends your entry at BE, it may offer a cleaner Leg 2, but it must confirm structure again.

## Step 7 - Interaction Termination

Trigger if any:

- Participation fails.
- Structure fails.
- Timing fails.
- Sweep fails.
- Opposite-side close occurs.

On termination:

- Rejection Mode OFF.
- Trade Mode OFF.
- Delete all structure.
- Delete Leg 1.
- Delete Leg 2.
- Delete timing.
- Delete sweep logic.
- Clear all pre_activation_probe_boundary state.
- System State = NEUTRAL RESET.

Rule:

- Once dead, cannot reuse.

Mode Transition Rule:

- Rejection Mode = OFF.
- System State = NEUTRAL RESET.
- No continuation or bypass retest state is carried forward.
- New setup requires fresh valid liquidity interaction.

Global interaction termination:

- If price exits active liquidity zone without initiating or progressing valid structural development, interaction is dead/invalid.
- Immediately terminate current 0-6 cycle.
- Reset system state.
- Return to Step 1 Gateway Rule.

Exit definition:

- Price closes outside active liquidity zone and no valid participation or structural development has occurred.
- OR structure has clearly failed or been invalidated.

Structural Exit Exception:

- If price exits liquidity zone after valid participation has occurred, movement may represent Leg 1 or ongoing structural development, and interaction remains active.

Termination conditions before structure completion:

- Price leaves liquidity zone without required sweep for stack.
- No participation within allowed candle window.
- Structure begins but fails and price exits zone.
- Clean continuation away from level without forming structure.
- Opposite-side break before setup completes.

After termination:

- Current interaction discarded completely.
- No structure carries forward.
- No bias retained.
- Return to neutral evaluation state.
- Return to Step 1.
- Re-evaluate current location.
- Wait for next valid close into liquidity.
- Begin new 0-6 cycle.

Key principle:

- Each liquidity interaction is independent.
- If it does not complete required behavior, it is void and has no impact on future setups.

## Step 10 - Trade Management

10.1 Risk Model:

- Maximum risk per trade = 10%.
- ATR source = 1-minute ATR(14) at moment of entry.
- Stop distance = greater of 1-minute ATR(14) or distance required to clear structure extreme by at least 2 ticks.
- Long stop below structure extreme + 2 ticks.
- Short stop above structure extreme + 2 ticks.
- If stop > ATR, reduce position size.
- Total trade risk must remain <= 10%.
- Stop is defined at entry.
- No discretionary widening after entry.

10.2 Order Placement:

- Immediately upon entry, place bracket order.
- Initial stop loss from 10.1.
- Primary target = +1 ATR from entry.
- Orders must function as one unified management set.

10.3 Early Protection:

Move stop to BE when first of:

- Price reaches +0.5 ATR intrabar.
- 3 candles pass after entry.

BE = entry price.

10.4 Break Even:

- BE is intermediate state, not final outcome.
- Trade remains active after BE.
- Trade continues toward TP1 or stop.

10.5 Primary Target:

- At +1 ATR from entry, close 50% position.
- Remaining 50% becomes Runner.
- After TP1, move stop on remaining 50% from BE back to original ATR-based stop.
- 50% locked at +1 ATR.
- Remaining 50% has full ATR room.
- Net trade risk = neutral.

10.6 Runner:

- Remaining 50% = Runner.
- Runner remains open until stop is hit or 12:00 PM hard cutoff.
- No trailing stop.
- No discretionary adjustment.
- No additional targets.

10.7 Time-Based Exit:

At 12:00 PM:

- Close all open positions immediately.
- Cancel all working orders.
- No new trades allowed.

10.8 Management Priority:

If multiple events occur within same candle, evaluate confirmed events only:

- BE trigger.
- TP1 fill +1 ATR.
- Stop execution.

10.9 Trade Completion Outcomes:

- Full Stop: initial stop hit before BE or TP1.
- Break Even: BE triggered and price returns to entry.
- Partial + Runner: 50% taken at +1 ATR, remaining 50% managed until stop or time exit.
- Time Exit: runner still open at 12:00 PM, position closed at cutoff.

10.10 Mandatory Session Close:

- No positions held past session.
- No swing holds.
- No overnight exposure.
- No exceptions.

10.11 State Freeze / Evidence:

System may record only confirmed events:

- Level touched.
- Order triggered.
- Target filled.
- Stop filled.
- Time cutoff reached.

Not allowed:

- Assuming future candle path.
- Assuming continuation.
- Assuming stop/target hit without confirmation.
- Projecting outcome.

Core law:

- Once a new state is reached, stop evaluation until next event is visibly confirmed.

Step 10 summary:

- Risk = 10% max.
- Stop = max(ATR, structure + 2 ticks).
- BE = first of +0.5 ATR or 3 candles.
- TP1 = +1 ATR, take 50%.
- After TP1, stop returns to original ATR stop.
- Runner = stop or 12:00 PM.
- 12:00 PM = flat, done.

## Pullback Continuation Pathways

Pullback continuation pathways are pathway selectors only.

They do NOT define new Step 4, Step 5, or Step 6 logic.
All structure and entry logic is handled by Steps 4-6.

Core Law:

- Mode changes the lane.
- Steps 4-6 still drive the car.

### S/R Pullback Continuation

Purpose:

- Convert failed support breakdown / S/R pullback condition into SHORT pullback continuation.
- Not a reversal model.

Direction:

- Direction = SHORT only.

Condition:

- Condition starts with a failure candle below the level.

Activation:

- Pathway activates when a reclaim candle returns above the level.
- pathway_activation_type = wick or close.

Candle assignment:

- Failure candle starts the condition.
- Reclaim candle activates the pathway.
- Reclaim candle becomes Candle A (Step 4).
- Candle B forms after Candle A and must remain above the level (no closes below).

Rule:

- Candle A MUST be the reclaim candle for close-based activation.
- The failure candle can never be used as Candle A.

Structure requirements:

- Structure must form above the level.
- Uses HH structure.
- structure_side_requirement = ABOVE_LEVEL.

State:

- candidate_modes includes S/R.
- pathway_level = active level.
- reclaim_candle_a is assigned for close-based activation.
- provisional_candle_a is assigned only if wick-based activation applies.
- sr_pathway_closed tracks pathway closure.

### R/S Pullback Continuation

Purpose:

- Convert failed resistance breakout / R/S pullback condition into LONG pullback continuation.
- Not a reversal model.

Direction:

- Direction = LONG only.

Condition:

- Condition starts with a failure candle above the level.

Activation:

- Pathway activates when a reclaim candle returns below the level.
- Step 2 activation: price interacts with resistance (touch or wick sufficient).
- pathway_activation_type = wick or close.

Candle assignment:

- Failure candle starts the condition.
- Reclaim candle activates the pathway.
- Reclaim candle becomes Candle A (Step 4).
- Candle B forms after Candle A and must remain below the level (no closes above).

Rule:

- Candle A MUST be the reclaim candle for close-based activation.
- The failure candle can never be used as Candle A.

Structure requirements:

- Structure must form below the level.
- Uses LL structure.
- structure_side_requirement = BELOW_LEVEL.
- Leg 2 must confirm within BELOW-level structure context.
- Any close back above the level invalidates the pathway.

State:

- candidate_modes includes R/S.
- pathway_level = active level.
- reclaim_candle_a is assigned for close-based activation.
- provisional_candle_a is assigned only if wick-based activation applies.
- rs_pathway_closed tracks pathway closure.

### Pathway Control

Pathway Lifecycle:

- Candidate -> Active (after Step 4) -> Consumed (after Step 6) -> Closed (if structure fails).

- Normal Rejection Mode remains default if no S/R or R/S activation condition exists.
- Trade eligibility requires completion of Step 4, Step 5, and Step 6.
- First opportunity only.
- If structure fails, the active pathway is closed.
- No re-attempt.
- No reinterpretation.
- If multiple pathways are active, the first pathway to complete valid Step 5 structure becomes controlling_mode.
- Once controlling_mode is assigned, all other pathways freeze.
- One interaction still allows only one entry.
